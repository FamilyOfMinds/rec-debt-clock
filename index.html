About REC Clock

What is REC Debt?

The REC Debt Clock tracks the real-time gap between US electricity consumption and renewable energy retirements. A positive deficit (in red) means America is consuming more electricity than its renewable energy certificates can cover.

Why This Matters

While America has committed to clean energy targets, the grid still relies on fossil fuels to bridge the gap. This clock visualizes that gap in real-time, powered by hourly data from the U.S. Energy Information Administration.

How It Works

  • Consumption: Total US electricity demand across 30+ balancing authorities
  • REC Retirements: Baseline of 725 TWh/year (NREL, EPA GATS average)
  • Deficit: Consumption minus retirements—the energy gap

Data Source

Real-time data comes from the EIA Form EIA-930 API (Hourly Electric Grid Monitor), covering 2019-present with 30+ US balancing authorities.
